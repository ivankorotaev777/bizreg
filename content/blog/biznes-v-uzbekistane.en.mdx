## Business forms available to foreigners

A foreign founder — an individual or a company — usually chooses between two main formats.

<CompareGrid>
  <CompareCard title="LLC" badge="standard form">
    A limited liability company — the universal option for most cases: trade, services, IT, consulting.
  </CompareCard>
  <CompareCard title="Foreign enterprise (ИП ООО)" badge="100% foreign capital" highlight>
    A fully-fledged LLC owned 100% by a foreigner. Many clients come to us for exactly this — a "clean" foreign legal entity in Uzbekistan.
  </CompareCard>
</CompareGrid>

<Callout type="warning" title="Careful with the term “ИП”">
A foreigner **cannot** register as a **sole proprietor (ИП)** in Uzbekistan — that form is only for citizens and residents. If you searched for "register an ИП", you most likely need an LLC or a foreign enterprise.
</Callout>

## Company registration, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Name & check">Choose a company name and verify its uniqueness in the registry.</Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Founding documents">Prepare the charter and the resolution (minutes) of incorporation.</Step>

  <Step n={4} title="State registration">File the documents to register the legal entity (remotely is possible).</Step>
  <Step n={5} title="Bank account">Open a current account — usually requires an in-person visit by the founder or director.</Step>
  <Step n={6} title="Tax registration">Register for tax and choose a suitable regime.</Step>
</Steps>

## Taxes and IT Park benefits

Uzbekistan offers several tax regimes, and tech companies can obtain the preferential **IT Park resident** status with reduced rates.

<Callout type="tip" title="If you're in IT">
For software development, IT services, and export of digital services, IT Park residency is often the most advantageous path. See the terms on [it-park.uz](https://it-park.uz).
</Callout>

Specific rates and thresholds are set by the Tax Code and updated annually — check current values on [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674893) and [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz). We deliberately avoid quoting numbers that may go out of date: on a consultation we'll match the regime to your activity under the current law.
