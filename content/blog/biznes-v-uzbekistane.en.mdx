Over the past few years Uzbekistan has become one of the most convenient jurisdictions in the region for foreign business: clear registration procedures, preferential tax regimes, and access to international payments. Here is a step-by-step overview for foreigners.

## Business forms available to foreigners

A foreign founder (individual or company) usually chooses one of:

- **LLC** — limited liability company, the standard form for most cases.
- **Foreign enterprise (ИП ООО)** — an LLC with 100% foreign capital. Many clients come to us specifically for this: a fully-fledged legal entity owned by a foreigner.

> Note: a foreigner **cannot** register as a **sole proprietor (ИП)** in Uzbekistan — that form is only for citizens and residents. If you searched for "register an ИП", you most likely need an LLC or a foreign enterprise.

## Company registration steps

1. **Choose a name** and check its uniqueness.
2. **Prepare founding documents** — charter, resolution/minutes of incorporation.
3. **Legal address** — the company needs a registered address (not a mass-registration address, to avoid questions from banks and authorities).
4. **State registration** — file the documents (remotely is possible).
5. **Open a bank account** — usually requires an in-person visit.
6. **Tax registration** and choosing a tax regime.

## Tax regimes and benefits

Uzbekistan offers several tax regimes, and IT companies can obtain the preferential **IT Park resident** status with reduced rates. Specific rates and thresholds are set by the Tax Code and updated annually — check current values on [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674893) and [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz), and IT Park terms on [it-park.uz](https://it-park.uz).

If your activity is software development, IT services, or export of digital services, IT Park residency is often the most advantageous path.

## What registration in Uzbekistan gives you

- Legal access to international settlements with clients and contractors.
- The ability to work with foreign counterparties when direct payments from your home country are difficult.
- Preferential tax regimes (including IT Park for tech companies).
- A fast launch — a company can realistically be opened quickly.
