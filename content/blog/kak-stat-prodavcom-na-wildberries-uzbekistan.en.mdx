## How to become a Wildberries seller in Uzbekistan?

In short: you need to set up a business in Uzbekistan (an LLC is more practical), open a bank account, register a cabinet on WB Partners, upload documents, add Wildberries to the list of commission agents on the tax services portal, and choose a work scheme. A foreigner cannot sell as a private individual — the platform works only with a registered business. Below we cover each step in detail.

## Why does a non-resident need a company, not just an account?

A seller account on WB Partners is opened only for a business entity registered in Uzbekistan — a sole proprietor or a legal entity. A private individual who is a foreigner cannot open one. It is like renting a spot at a market: first you secure the right to trade here, then you lay out your goods.

For a non-resident this means three tasks: set up a business, open a bank account in an Uzbek bank, and prepare documents for the platform. It is easier to solve them correctly at once than to redo everything later.

## Which business form should a non-resident choose — sole proprietor or LLC?

For a non-resident seller an **LLC** is more practical, for two reasons.

| Criterion | Sole proprietor | LLC |
|---|---|---|
| Residence permit in Uzbekistan | usually a residence status is required | not required |
| Import/export operations | not available | allowed |
| Remote registration | limited | yes, via a representative |

The first reason — **an LLC needs no residence registration or permit**: the company can be set up without moving to the country. The second, crucial for a marketplace, is that **import/export operations are allowed only to a legal entity**. A seller's goods almost always come from abroad: some from Russia, some from China and other countries. A sole proprietor cannot run such imports — only an LLC can. So even if turnover alone would fit a sole proprietorship at first, imports still tip the choice toward an LLC.

For more on company registration by a foreigner, see our guide on the [foreign enterprise in Uzbekistan](/blog/inostrannoe-predpriyatie).

## How to register on WB Partners: 6 steps

1. **Set up a business in Uzbekistan.** Register an LLC, obtain a taxpayer ID and a legal address. For a non-resident this is done remotely via a representative.
2. **Open a bank account.** Wildberries transfers proceeds to the company's account in an Uzbek bank — you open it using the LLC's documents.
3. **Register a cabinet on WB Partners.** Go to [wildberries.uz](https://seller.wildberries.ru/) → "For business" → "Become a seller", confirm your phone number, create a cabinet.
4. **Upload documents.** The founder's passport, the LLC registration certificate and bank details. After a check, the cabinet is activated.
5. **Add Wildberries as a commission agent.** On the [my.soliq.uz](https://my.soliq.uz) portal, add the platform to the list of commission agents. A mandatory Uzbek step — without it settlements and reporting will be incorrect.
6. **Choose a scheme and start selling.** The main scheme for Uzbekistan is FBW: goods go to the WB warehouse, and the marketplace handles storage and delivery.

The first two steps are the registrar's job; the rest you do in your cabinet. That is why it is convenient for a foreigner to outsource company and account setup and focus on the product and sales.

## What taxes does a marketplace seller pay in Uzbekistan?

The small-business regime in Uzbekistan is one of the mildest in the region. While turnover is below the VAT threshold, you pay a simple turnover tax rather than complex VAT.

| Tax | 2026 rate | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Turnover tax | from 4% | turnover below the VAT threshold (≈5 billion soums) |
| VAT (standard) | 12% | taxable operations on the domestic market |
| VAT (voluntary) | 6% | trade, catering, services, from 01.06.2026 |
| VAT (export) | 0% | with documentary proof of export |
| Corporate profit tax | 15% | base rate for an LLC |
| Dividends | 10% | payments to non-resident participants |

An important detail for a new company: **first-time profit-tax payers are exempt from it for the first tax period** (from 1 January 2026, except tax on dividends and interest). For a starting seller this is a tangible saving in the first year. Detailed breakdowns are in our articles on [taxes in Uzbekistan](/blog/nalogi-v-uzbekistane), [VAT](/blog/nds-v-uzbekistane) and [corporate profit tax](/blog/nalog-na-pribyl-v-uzbekistane).

## Does a non-resident director need a work permit and visa?

Yes. If the company's general director is a non-resident, they will need a **work permit and a visa**. These are separate procedures handled after the LLC is registered. It is best to plan them right away so the director can legally manage the company, sign documents and open the account. If the director is an Uzbek citizen or a holder of a residence permit, a work permit is not required — that is also a possible structure.

## What do we need from you for registration?

No trips and no queues — everything is arranged remotely. From you we need:

- a scan of the founder's passport (and the director's, if they are different people);
- the desired company name and type of activity;
- the category of goods you plan to sell — to account for labeling and platform specifics.

Everything else — registration, the account, documents, the director's work permit and visa — is handled by the registrar.

## Can I sell on Uzum and Ozon with the same company?

Yes. A company registered in Uzbekistan works for all marketplaces at once. The mechanism is the same everywhere: a legal entity, a bank account and adding the platform to the list of commission agents on the tax services portal. With the same company you can enter both **Uzum Market** and **Ozon** — no need to open a new business per platform. This is convenient if you want to avoid depending on a single platform and sell on several at once.

## Common mistakes

Trying to register on WB as a private individual without a company; choosing a sole proprietorship and hitting the ban on importing goods; forgetting to add Wildberries as a commission agent on my.soliq.uz and getting incorrect reporting; overlooking the work permit and visa for a non-resident director; taking tax rates from outdated blogs — VAT rules changed on 1 June 2026. Verify facts on [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz) — or leave registration and the marketplace launch to us.
