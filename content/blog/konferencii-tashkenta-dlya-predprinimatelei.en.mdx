## Why should a founder attend conferences in Tashkent?

The short answer — for the people and the context. Three reasons it pays off:

- **Contacts in two days instead of six months.** One hall gathers investors, future partners, suppliers and the officials who make decisions. A live introduction beats any cold outreach.
- **First-hand read of the market.** On panels and in the hallways you hear where Uzbekistan's market is heading: which sectors grow, where new incentives appear, what changes in the law.
- **Visibility for your business.** If you are just entering the market, a conference is a cheap way to be seen by the right audience.
- **Access to government support and incentives.** Development institutions — IT Park, investment agencies, banks — all gather in one place at these forums. It is a direct chance to learn about active support programmes and ask questions to the people who run them.
- **Immersion in the local culture and business mindset.** In Central Asia, business is built on trust and personal acquaintance: jumping straight to the deal is not the norm — you first need to meet, talk and become a familiar face. The regional business culture is largely collectivist: partners are chosen not only by the terms of a deal, but by how comfortable it feels to work together. A conference is exactly the environment where those relationships form naturally — over lunch, in the hallways, at a shared session. You cannot absorb this from a screen; you have to come and experience it. Founders who pick up on this dynamic close deals faster.

For a foreign founder this is especially valuable: you quickly grasp how things work here and build relationships before you even open a company. How to open one without mistakes — we cover in detail in [how a foreigner opens a company in Uzbekistan](/blog/inostrannoe-predpriyatie).

### ICT Week Uzbekistan — the country's main tech forum

The flagship event, run by **IT Park Uzbekistan** together with the Ministry of Digital Technologies. Held annually since 2004, in 2026 it runs **22–25 September at the CAEx exhibition complex** in Tashkent. It combines several events under one roof: an IT exhibition, a startup summit by IT Park, a fintech forum and an AI forum. It draws technology companies, investors, telecom operators and startups from across the region.

**Who should go:** IT companies, startups, investors, anyone planning a digital business in Uzbekistan. If you are looking at [IT Park residency and its tax benefits](/blog/it-park-lgoty), this event is a must. Official site — [ictweek.uz](https://ictweek.uz).

### Tashkent International Investment Forum (TIIF) — about big money

Central Asia's leading investment platform. In 2026 it runs **16–19 June**. The scale is impressive: in 2025 the forum gathered over 8,000 participants from nearly a hundred countries, with signed contracts exceeding 30 billion dollars. The format is panel discussions, business-to-government meetings and a large sector exhibition.

**Who should go:** those seeking investment, major partners, or entering the market with a serious project. Official site — [tiif.online](https://tiif.online).

### InnoWeek — the week of innovation

Uzbekistan's flagship innovation forum under the Agency for Innovative Development. In 2026 it runs **27–29 October at CAEx**. The focus is artificial intelligence, digital transformation, biotech, green energy and engineering.

**Who should go:** tech and science projects, teams looking for grants and innovation partnerships. Official site — [innoweek.uz](https://innoweek.uz).

### Central Eurasian Venture Forum (CEVF) — venture and startups

A venture forum that gathers investors and founders around "smart capital" and AI companies. In 2026 it took place in spring (3 April) and gathered over 800 investors and entrepreneurs — it is an annual event, so the next one is expected in 2027, closer to spring.

**Who should go:** startups seeking investment and anyone interested in the region's venture market. Official site — [ventureforum.asia](https://ventureforum.asia).

### Women in Tech Awards Central Asia — women's leadership in tech

An annual event in Tashkent (October) for women leaders, founders, researchers and startups. It is both an award and a networking platform where new names and projects stand out.

**Who should go:** women founders and teams for whom diversity and visibility in the tech scene matter.

## How to get the most out of a conference?

The ticket and the entrance are only half the job. A few simple rules that turn a trip into results:

- **Register in advance.** Large forums require registration, and it often closes weeks before the event.
- **Set a goal.** Three needed contacts or one investor meeting already makes a conference a success. A vague "let's have a look" gives nothing.
- **Prepare a short pitch about yourself.** 30 seconds, clear and to the point: who you are, what you do, what you are looking for.
- **Collect contacts and write the next day.** The real work begins after the conference — in follow-up, while people still remember you.
