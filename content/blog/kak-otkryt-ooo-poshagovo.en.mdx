<Callout type="warning" title="About the term «IP» (sole proprietor)">
If you searched for "open an IP in Uzbekistan", note this: a [sole proprietorship (IP)](/en/blog/ip-v-uzbekistane-dlya-inostrancev) is available to a foreigner only with residency in the country (registration by place of residence or a residence permit) and with limits, so a non-resident usually opens an LLC. Without residency, foreigners use an **LLC** or a **foreign enterprise** (the same LLC, but with 100% foreign capital). This article is about the LLC.

## How do you open an LLC in Uzbekistan step by step?

This is the core of the article. Follow the steps in order — each depends on the previous one.

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Name and its check">
  Choose a company name and check it for uniqueness in the register of legal entities. The name must not duplicate an already registered one and must not contain restricted words (for example, unfounded references to government bodies). **Common mistake:** filing with a taken name and losing time on a repeat submission. Prepare 2–3 backup options.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Founding documents">
  You prepare the LLC charter and the decision of the sole founder (or the minutes of the general meeting if there are several founders). The charter sets out the name, address, activities, the size and distribution of charter capital shares, and the management structure. **Nuance:** documents of a foreign corporate founder may require legalization/apostille and a notarized translation. **Mistake:** downloading a "universal charter" from the internet — it often ignores foreign participation and surfaces during checks.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Legal address">
  The company needs a real legal address for registration. Critically, it **must not be a "mass-registration" address** (where dozens of firms are registered) — the tax authority and banks treat such addresses with suspicion, which directly delays opening an account and triggers checks. **Nuance:** you usually need a letter of guarantee from the property owner. **Mistake:** taking the cheapest "address for registration" and then failing bank compliance.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="State registration">
  The document package is submitted for state registration of the legal entity — this can also be done remotely through the unified government services portal (my.gov.uz). After registration the company is entered into the register and assigned a tax ID (INN) and registration details. **Nuance:** founder and address data must match exactly across all documents — a one-letter discrepancy leads to rejection. **Mistake:** typos in the transliteration of a foreigner's name versus the passport.
  </Step>
  <Step n={5} title="Bank account">
  After registration you open a settlement account. At most banks this requires an **in-person visit** by the founder or director — typically the longest stage of the whole process. The bank runs compliance: it reviews the founding documents, the address, the source of funds and the nature of the business. **Nuance:** check the chosen bank's requirements for non-residents and the needed documents in advance. **Mistake:** underestimating the timeline and planning the trip too tightly.
  </Step>
  <Step n={6} title="Tax registration and choosing a regime">
  The company registers for tax and selects a tax regime. The general regime applies by default, but with suitable turnover you can choose the simplified one. IT companies separately apply for IT Park resident status. **Nuance:** choose the regime deliberately at the start, not "by inertia". **Mistake:** missing the deadlines for tax registration or the first filing and incurring fines.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="What's fast and what's slow">
Document preparation and state registration are relatively quick when the package is ready. The real bottleneck is the bank account: the in-person visit and compliance stretch out the timeline. Plan your trip specifically around this step.
</Callout>

## LLC taxes in 2026: what to know up front

The tax regime determines the burden on your business, so choose it deliberately back at step 6.

<CompareGrid>
  <CompareCard title="General regime" badge="profit + VAT">
  Profit tax of **15%** and VAT of **12%**. Suited to companies with larger turnover and VAT-registered counterparties. Accurate as of 2026-06-16.
  </CompareCard>
  <CompareCard title="Simplified regime" badge="turnover tax" highlight>
  A **4%** turnover tax instead of profit tax and VAT — simpler accounting, convenient for small business and services. Works while turnover stays within the threshold. Accurate as of 2026-06-16.
  </CompareCard>
</CompareGrid>

<Callout type="info" title="On VAT and thresholds (from 01.06.2026)">
From 1 June 2026 the mandatory VAT registration threshold is **12,000 BRV** (roughly about 5 billion soum). A voluntary reduced rate of **6%** also applies to trade and services. These figures are sensitive to change — verify the current edition on [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz) and [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674893). Accurate as of 2026-06-16.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="If you run an IT company">
For software development and the export of digital services, the most advantageous option is often **IT Park resident status** — it carries a preferential tax regime (benefits extended through 2028). Conditions and the list of eligible activities are on [it-park.uz](https://it-park.uz). Accurate as of 2026-06-16.
</Callout>

## Can you open an LLC remotely?

Most of the path can genuinely be done remotely: the name check, preparing the founding documents and filing for state registration through the government services portal. The main exception is the **bank account**: most banks require the founder or director to be present in person, so a single trip is usually planned around opening the account. If visiting is impossible, options with a power of attorney and a bank chosen for remote onboarding may exist — but this depends on the specific bank's policy and your situation.

## After opening: what you must do next

Registering the LLC is only the beginning. From there the entity must keep accounting and tax records, file reports on time and pay taxes under the chosen regime. For most foreign founders, outsourcing accounting is more convenient: it's cheaper than an in-house accountant and removes the risk of fines for late filings. If you plan to hire staff, you'll need to formalize employment and payroll taxes, and a foreign director will usually need a work permit.
