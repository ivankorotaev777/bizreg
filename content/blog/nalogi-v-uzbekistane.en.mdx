## VAT

The standard VAT rate is **12%**. A business becomes a VAT payer on the general regime (or when exceeding the turnover threshold). Exports are zero-rated upon confirmation. Deduction and exemption nuances are in the Tax Code.

## Profit tax

Base rate — **15%**. Paid on profit (income minus deductible expenses), not on total revenue. Applies to companies on the general regime.

## Personal income & social tax

On salaries: **PIT 12%** + **social tax 12%** (25% for budget organizations). IT Park residents get a reduced PIT (see below).

## Turnover tax (simplified)

Small businesses below a turnover threshold may pay **turnover tax** on revenue, base rate **4%** (other rates for some activities). Simpler than VAT + profit tax. Check the current threshold on [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz).

## Reporting and deadlines

Tax returns are filed periodically: for most taxes monthly or quarterly, depending on the tax and regime, with payment due by set deadlines. Late filing leads to penalties and interest, so it's important to keep records regularly and not miss dates. The exact tax calendar, forms and filing procedure are on the [soliq.uz](https://soliq.uz) portal. If you'd rather not track all this, we keep the books and watch the deadlines for you — preparing and filing returns on time and standing behind the calculations.

VAT registration deserves separate attention: on the general regime or when exceeding the turnover threshold, a company must register as a VAT payer and issue invoices in the required format. We help with registration and set up correct document flow from the first month.

## IT Park benefits — why it pays off

<Callout type="tip" title="IT Park residents">
IT Park resident companies are exempt from **profit tax, VAT, social tax and turnover tax** (preferential regime until 1 January 2028), and **employee PIT is 7.5%**. For software development and export of digital services this is often the most advantageous path. Terms and eligible activities — on [it-park.uz](https://it-park.uz).
</Callout>
